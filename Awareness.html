Awareness & Online Safety

Learn how to use scholarship information responsibly and protect yourself from common online scams.

🚨

Stay Alert Against Scholarship Scams

  • Be Caution of messages offering guarantee scholarships in exchange for a fee.
  • Never upload personal or sensitive documents to unverified websites.
  • Avoid clicking on suspicious links or downloading unknown files.
  • Always verify scholarship information through official government portals.
🛡️

Keep Your Account Secure

  • Never share your password ,OTP,or login credentials with anyone.
  • Create a strong and unique password for your Account.(Containing combinations of alphabets,numbers, and special characters )
  • Always log out after using your account on a shared or public device.
  • Check the website address carefully before entering your personal information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Scholarship Saathi an official government portal?

No. Scholarship Saathi is a CEP awareness project. Applications should be made through the relevant official government portal.

Can this website guarantee that I qualify?

No. The website provides guidance only. The official scholarship rules determine eligibility.

What should I do if someone asks for my OTP?

Never share your OTP with anyone.if a request seems suspicious or unexpected do not proceed and verify it through an official source.
