How to Apply

A simple general guide to understanding an online scholarship application.

01

Find a Scholarship

Explore scholarships that may match your education, state and circumstances.

02

Check Eligibility

Read the current official eligibility rules carefully before applying.

03

Register

Create an account on the official scholarship portal.

04

Complete the Form

Enter accurate academic and personal information.

05

Upload Documents

Upload only the documents requested by the selected scholarship.

06

Submit and Track

Review the application and use the official portal to track its status.

Common Document Checklist

🪪 Identity Proof

Click here to view commonly required identity documents.

Commonly Accepted Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Voter ID Card
  • Passport
  • Driving Licence

⚠️ The required identity document may vary depending on the scholarship scheme. Always verify the current requirements on the official scholarship portal.

📚 Academic Records

Click here to view commonly required academic documents.

Commonly Required Academic Documents

  • Previous Year Marksheet
  • Current Course / Semester Marksheet
  • Passing Certificate
  • Bonafide Certificate
  • Admission Receipt / Fee Receipt
  • College ID Card

⚠️ Academic documents may differ depending on the scholarship scheme and course. Always check the current requirements on the official scholarship portal.

💰 Income Proof

Click here to view commonly required income documents.

Commonly Required Income Documents

  • Income Certificate
  • Annual Family Income Certificate
  • Income Proof issued by the competent authority
  • Salary Slip, where applicable
  • Income Tax Return (ITR), where applicable

⚠️ Income requirements and acceptable documents vary by scholarship scheme. Always check the current requirements on the official scholarship portal.

🏦 Bank Details

Click here to view commonly required bank documents.

Commonly Required Bank Documents

  • Bank Passbook
  • Bank Account Details
  • Account Holder Name
  • Bank Account Number
  • IFSC Code

⚠️ Make sure the bank account details belong to the eligible student or the account holder specified by the scholarship scheme. Never share your ATM PIN, CVV, OTP, or internet banking password.

🏠 Residence Proof

Click here to view commonly required residence documents.

Commonly Required Residence Documents

  • Domicile Certificate
  • Residence Certificate
  • Address Proof
  • Ration Card, where applicable
  • Electricity Bill, where applicable
  • Other government-issued address proof

⚠️ Residence requirements may vary depending on the scholarship scheme. Always check the current requirements on the official scholarship portal.

📜 Category Certificate

Click here to view commonly required category documents.

Commonly Required Category Documents

  • Caste Certificate
  • Non-Creamy Layer Certificate, where applicable
  • EWS Certificate, where applicable
  • Validity Certificate, where applicable
  • Category / Community Certificate, where applicable

⚠️ Category-related requirements vary according to the scholarship scheme and applicant category. Always verify the current requirements on the official scholarship portal.

⚠️ Requirements and deadlines can change. Always check the official scholarship instructions before submitting.
